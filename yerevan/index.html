About

A New York practice,
now in Yerevan

Baseline Psychotherapy was founded in New York in 2019, is active in Singapore, and is now expanding globally, with Yerevan as its first new city. The reason is simple: Armenia's international community is growing faster than the English-language mental health support available to it. I have relocated six times across three continents myself, so the work this community needs is work I have lived.

I am J.R. Hernandez, psychotherapist, specialized in burnout and mood disorders: trained in Medical Neuroscience at Duke University School of Medicine, with the American Psychological Association's specialization in anxiety and mood disorders and a master's degree in emotional intelligence. My practice holds a 5.0 rating across 22 Google reviews, with clients in more than 20 countries.

Therapy in Yerevan

Therapy for the life
you actually live here

Relocation looks like logistics and feels like identity. Most of my clients in Yerevan's international community are professionals, founders, and families who keep performing while quietly running on empty: the move went "fine", but the energy never came back. That pattern has a clinical name, and it responds to structured treatment.

Sessions address burnout, anxiety, depression, emotional dysregulation, life transition, and relationship strain, in native English or Spanish. The method is process-based: a clinical assessment first, then a plan aimed at what keeps the problem in place, with progress measured session by session.
